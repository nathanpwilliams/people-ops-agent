# Expenses

Business expense and reimbursement policy for Northwind Robotics.

## 1 What is reimbursable

Northwind Robotics reimburses reasonable, necessary expenses incurred while doing company business, including business travel, meals with customers or candidates, software and hardware approved by your manager, and professional memberships related to your role.

## 2 Submitting an expense

Submit expenses through the expense tool within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be declined. Attach an itemized receipt for any single expense over $25. Each expense needs a short business purpose, for example "customer dinner, Acme site visit".

## 3 Approval and payment

Your manager reviews and approves expense reports. Approved expenses are reimbursed by Finance on the next payroll run, normally within 10 business days of approval. Finance may ask for additional documentation before paying.

## 4 Travel

Book economy-class flights and standard hotel rooms. Hotel rates should not exceed $250 per night without prior approval from your manager. Meals while traveling are reimbursed at actual cost up to $75 per day. Use ride-share or public transit where practical; rental cars require manager approval.

## 5 Non-reimbursable items

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol except at approved customer or team events, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, upgrades to business or first class, expenses for family members or travel companions, and purchases made without a receipt over $25.

## 6 Company cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year may request a company credit card from Finance. Company card transactions must still be submitted in the expense tool with receipts within 30 days.
