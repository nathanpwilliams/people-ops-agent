# Benefits

Overview of employee benefits at Northwind Robotics. Plan documents in the HR portal are the authoritative source for coverage details.

## 1 Health insurance

Medical, dental, and vision coverage is available to all full-time employees. Coverage begins on your first day of employment; there is no waiting period. The company pays 100% of the employee premium and 75% of the premium for dependents. Enrollment forms are due within 30 days of your start date.

## 2 Retirement plan

Employees may contribute to the company 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Northwind Robotics matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary. Company matching contributions vest immediately.

## 3 Life and disability insurance

The company provides basic life insurance equal to one year of base salary and long-term disability coverage at 60% of base salary, at no cost to the employee. Supplemental coverage can be purchased during open enrollment.

## 4 Parental leave

All new parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first 12 months after birth, adoption, or placement. Coordinate the timing with your manager and People Ops at least 30 days in advance when possible.

## 5 Learning and development stipend

Each full-time employee has a $1,200 annual learning stipend for courses, books, conferences, and certifications related to their role. Submit stipend expenses through the expense tool and tag them "L&D".

## 6 Changing your benefits

Open enrollment takes place every November for coverage starting January 1. Outside open enrollment, you may change your elections within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.
