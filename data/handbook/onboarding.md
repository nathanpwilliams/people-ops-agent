# Onboarding

Onboarding process for new Northwind Robotics employees and their managers.

## 1 Before your first day

Once your offer is signed, People Ops sends the new-hire paperwork and background-check request. Complete both within 5 business days. IT ships your laptop to arrive before your start date. Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy and sends a first-week schedule.

## 2 Your first day

Day one starts at 10:00 with a People Ops orientation covering the handbook, benefits enrollment, and tools. You will receive your accounts, badge, and a walkthrough of the office or remote setup. Your manager and buddy meet you after orientation.

## 3 Your first week

During the first week you meet your immediate team, review your role description with your manager, and agree a 30-60-90 day plan. Complete the required training modules (security awareness and code of conduct) within 10 business days of your start date.

## 4 Buddy program

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from outside their immediate team for the first 90 days. Your buddy is an informal guide for questions about how things work day to day. Buddies and new hires are encouraged to meet weekly for the first month.

## 5 90-day check-in

At the end of your first 90 days your manager schedules a check-in to review the 30-60-90 plan, gather your feedback on onboarding, and set goals for the rest of the year. This is a two-way conversation, not a performance review.

## 6 Standard onboarding checklist

Managers use the standard checklist in the HR portal for every new hire: paperwork complete (People Ops), laptop shipped (IT), accounts created (IT), buddy assigned (manager), first-week schedule sent (manager), benefits enrolled (employee, within 30 days), required training complete (employee, within 10 business days), and 30-60-90 plan agreed (manager and employee).
