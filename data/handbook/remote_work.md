# Remote Work

Hybrid and remote work policy for Northwind Robotics.

## 1 Eligibility and expectations

Northwind Robotics is a hybrid company. Most office-based roles are expected in the office 2 days per week, normally Tuesday and Thursday. Roles designated fully remote in the job description have no in-office requirement. Hardware, lab, and field roles follow the schedule set by their manager because of equipment needs.

## 2 Core hours

All employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours you may arrange your schedule with your manager. Post your working hours and time zone in your calendar and Slack profile.

## 3 Home office stipend

Full-time employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment, available after your first 30 days. In addition, the company reimburses up to $50 per month toward home internet. Submit both through the expense tool tagged "home office".

## 4 Equipment

The company provides a laptop and, where needed, a monitor and headset. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when you leave. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within 24 hours.

## 5 Working from another location

You may work from a location other than your home address, including another country, for up to 4 weeks per calendar year. Requests over 1 week require approval from your manager and People Ops at least 2 weeks in advance, because tax and data-protection rules vary by location.

## 6 Security

When working remotely, connect through the company VPN, lock your screen when away, and never work on confidential material in public spaces where screens can be seen. Use only company-managed devices for company data.
