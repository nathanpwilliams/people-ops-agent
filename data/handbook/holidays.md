# Holidays

Company holiday schedule for Northwind Robotics.

## 1 Paid company holidays

Northwind Robotics observes 11 paid company holidays each year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. The exact dates are published in the HR portal each December for the following year.

## 2 Holidays falling on a weekend

When a company holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the following Monday.

## 3 Floating holiday

In addition to the 11 company holidays, every full-time employee receives 1 floating holiday per calendar year, to be used for a religious, cultural, or personal day of significance. The floating holiday must be used within the calendar year and does not carry over. Request it through the HR portal like PTO.

## 4 Working on a holiday

If your manager asks you to work on a company holiday, you may take an alternate day off within 30 days. Record the alternate day in the HR portal with the note "holiday swap" so it is not deducted from your PTO balance.

## 5 Winter closure

The office is closed from December 26 through December 31. These closure days are paid and are not deducted from PTO. Employees in roles that require coverage during the closure will be scheduled by their manager and receive alternate days off in January.
