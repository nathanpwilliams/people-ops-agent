# PTO

Paid time off (vacation) policy for Northwind Robotics employees.

## 1 Eligibility

All regular full-time employees are eligible for paid time off (PTO) starting on their first day of employment. Part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week accrue PTO on a prorated basis. Contractors and interns are not eligible for PTO.

## 2.1 Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per calendar year, credited at a rate of 1.5 days per month on the first day of each month. New hires begin accruing in their first month. PTO is tracked in the HR portal, where you can see your available and pending days at any time.

## 2.2 Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days that is unused on December 31 is forfeited. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year.

## 3 Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests through the HR portal. For 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter requests should be submitted at least 2 business days ahead. Your manager will approve or decline the request within 3 business days. Approved time shows as "pending" until the dates have passed.

## 4 Sick time

Sick time is separate from PTO. Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year, available from the first day of employment. No advance notice is required, but please tell your manager as early as practical on the day. Sick days do not carry over.

## 5 Extended and unpaid leave

Requests for medical leave, disability accommodation, parental leave beyond the standard benefit, or unpaid leave are handled individually by the People team and are not processed through the standard PTO request flow. Contact People Ops directly to start a conversation.

## 6 PTO when leaving the company

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on the final paycheck where required by local law. Carried-over days are included in the payout. Pending requests for dates after your last day are cancelled.
